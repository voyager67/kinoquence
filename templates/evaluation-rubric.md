# Human review rubric (score each 1–5)

This is a review sheet, not an automated model benchmark. Use at least two reviewers for public comparisons where possible.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | No clear question or reason to continue | Some curiosity, predictable | Specific, immediately legible and relevant |
| Story logic | Disconnected beats | Mostly coherent | Strong cause/effect and payoff |
| Factual trust | Unsupported/misleading | Sources exist but need review | Claims accurately sourced and clearly framed |
| Visual clarity | Hard to read | Main idea is visible | Strong composition and phone readability |
| Motion | Random or static | Adequate | Motivated, smooth and paced with the story |
| Audio | Distracting or unbalanced | Usable | Clear voice, controlled music/SFX and clean mix |
| Accessibility | Missing/unreadable captions | Mostly readable | Accurate, synced and readable captions |
| Technical integrity | Export faults | Minor issues | Correct format, no visible faults after review |

Report the prompt, model/provider/version, generation settings, human reviewer count, median scores and disagreements. Never convert this rubric into a claim of universal superiority from one example.
